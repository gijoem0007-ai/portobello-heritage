Portobello Oral History Archive

--- text @top=346 left=150
Explore our digital archive below. Each chapter of Portobello’s history can be experienced in two ways: listen to the Audio Files, authentic recordings made in years gone by and restored for clarity, with nothing added or re-voiced; or watch our Video Essays, where those same genuine voices are illustrated with AI-assisted reconstructions of the people, places and eras described. The voices are real; the images are our reinterpretation of the period.
