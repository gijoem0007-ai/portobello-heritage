PHT Replica Check

Every page of the replica compared with the live Portobello Heritage Trust website, as a visitor sees them in a browser at desktop width. Checked 14 October 2026.

36pages compared
28match
8with differences
What was compared on each page
  • Text: every sentence a visitor can read, ignoring spacing and line breaks.
  • Pictures: matched by the picture itself, not its file name, so a different picture is caught even if the name is the same.
  • Buttons and links: their labels and where they lead (pages, PDFs, other websites).
  • Galleries and videos: how many galleries, how many photos in each, and which YouTube videos the page plays.
Left out on purpose (these differences are intended and recorded in the changelog)
  • The menus, already checked against the live site separately. The replica also shows the category menu on article pages.
  • The replica's footer note saying it is a local test copy.

Findings

Live site as published on 14 October 2026, compared with the replica rebuilt from the Xara export of 14 October 2026 (11:54).

Fixed or brought across since the first check

  • Fixed Photos of Coade Stone Pillars: the 12-photo grid with descriptions on hover, then the 8 zoomable close-ups, as on the live desktop page (13 October). Page details
  • Fixed Heritage: the underlined "Heritage" heading (13 October). Page details
  • Fixed Events: the extra "Forthcoming Events" button removed, and the greyed "Back to Events Page" button shown (13 October). Page details
  • Fixed Oral History: the new intro wording, from the 14 October export. Page details
  • Fixed Home: the new "Join the Portobello Heritage Trust…" paragraph, from the 14 October export. Its Membership Application Form button will appear once the forms are built. Page details

For Joe to confirm

  • Check Contact: the line "Postal address: Portobello Heritage Trust, 16 Bellfield Lane, Edinburgh, EH15 2BL" is no longer on the Contact page itself in the latest Xara version; it now appears only inside the membership form pop-up. Was that intended? (The Registered Office address is still on the home page.) Page details
  • Check Photos of Coade Stone Pillars on the live site: the top gallery's description area includes an image whose address is the placeholder "{text}". Hover over a photo there and see whether a small broken-image icon appears. Page details

Effects on the live site that the replica doesn't have

  • Effect Photos that fade in as you scroll down to them: Chocolate Factory, Helen Hopekirk, Heritage Trail Leaflet, Marine Gardens, Shops and Shopkeepers and Toddlers Hut. On the replica the same photos are there from the start. Page details

Not covered: the phone layout of the live site, and spacing or alignment that only a person looking at the page would notice. Intended differences (page titles, clickable addresses, plaque panels, payment details on Contact) are listed under each page, not as problems.
